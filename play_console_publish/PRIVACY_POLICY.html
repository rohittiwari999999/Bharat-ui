PosterBanao

Privacy Policy • Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PosterBanao: Festival & Ads ("we", "our", or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you handle within the application.

Summary: PosterBanao processes design images, personal branding details, and exported posters locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data or photos to third parties.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Personal Branding Information: When you set up your profile (Business Name, Owner Name, Phone Number, Designation, Email, and Website), this data is stored locally in your device storage to automatically render the footer and header branding strips on your poster designs.
  • Photos & Business Logos: Any photo or logo you pick from your phone's photo library is accessed using the Android Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`), which does not grant broad file access. Your photos remain on your device and are used exclusively to create the custom poster output.
  • Generated Graphic Exports: When you export a High-Definition poster (3000x4000 px), it is saved in your device's private cache and shared only when you explicitly choose to export or send it via WhatsApp, Instagram, or file manager.

3. Device Permissions

PosterBanao adheres to the principle of least-privilege access:

  • No Broad Storage Permissions: We do NOT request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. The Android Photo Picker handles user-selected images securely.
  • Internet Permission: Required solely to load online font glyphs, template catalogs, and communicate with Google Gemini API for creative quote suggestions.

4. Third-Party Services and AI Integration

The app integrates with the following reliable third-party infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services: For application licensing and secure app integrity.
  • Google Gemini API: Used when you tap "AI Quotes" to generate festival poetry and promotional slogans based on standard category keywords. No personal phone numbers or user images are transmitted to the AI model.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your branding and draft posters are stored locally on your device:

  • You can edit or erase your business profile details at any time from the app's Profile Setup screen.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored data and cached exports.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is intended for business marketing, festival greetings, and general audience graphic design.
