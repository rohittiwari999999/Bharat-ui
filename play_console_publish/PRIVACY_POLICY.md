# Privacy Policy for PosterBanao

**Last updated:** October 2026

## 1. Overview
PosterBanao ("we", "our", or "the app") provides graphic design and poster-making tools for Indian festivals, business branding, and personal invitations. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 2. Information Handled by the App
* **Local Business Profile:** User-entered details (Business Name, Owner Name, Designation, Phone Number, Email, Website) are stored locally on your device to automatically render branding footers and headers.
* **Photos & Logos:** Selected via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`). Images are processed on-device and never sent to our servers.
* **High-Resolution Exports:** Generated posters (3000x4000 px) are saved directly to your device storage.

## 3. Permissions
* **INTERNET:** Required for fetching online typography styles, templates, and connecting to the Google Gemini API for creative poetry and slogan suggestions.
* **No broad media permissions:** We do not request `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`.

## 4. Third-Party Services
* **Google Play Services:** For security, licensing, and app integrity.
* **Google Gemini API:** For generating contextual festive greetings and business quotes. No personal user data or photos are transmitted to the AI model.

## 5. Data Deletion
All profile data can be edited or wiped from within the app settings. Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally stored data.
